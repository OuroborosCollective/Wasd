# Economy and Matrix: The Flow of Power

The economy of Areloria is a closed-loop system where energy and value are intertwined.

## 1. Matrix Energy
The underlying "mana" of the world.
- Used for magical rituals, high-tier crafting, and world-level construction.
- Harvested from ley lines and specific world events.

## 2. Market Dynamics
Every city maintains its own market with supply and demand tracked deterministically.
- **Price Factors**: Scarcity, trade route safety, local taxes, and krieg (war) status.
- **Haggling**: NPC traders use their personality traits (Greed vs. Empathy) to adjust prices for individuals.

## 3. Factional Taxes and Governance
- Kingdoms and Guilds can set tax rates for their territories.
- These funds are used for public works (construction contracts) and military upkeep.

## 4. Resource Cycles
Resources are not infinite. They grow, decay, and are consumed based on the **Ouroboros Cycle**. Over-farming a region will lead to ecological collapse, requiring intervention from the World Brain or players.
